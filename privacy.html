Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reliable Breeze Air Conditioning & Refrigeration ("Reliable Breeze", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or contact us about a project. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

01Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a bid or contact us, you may provide your name, company, email address, phone number, project type and project details, along with any drawings or documents you choose to send.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit.

Preferences stored on your device. If you use the accessibility menu, your choices are saved in your browser so they carry across pages. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

02How we use information

  • To respond to bid requests and inquiries
  • To prepare proposals, schedule site visits and deliver our services
  • To communicate with you about a project or service agreement
  • To maintain, secure and improve this website
  • To comply with legal, licensing and insurance obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

03How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run our business:

  • Service providers such as website hosting, email and business software vendors, who may only use it to provide services to us
  • Project partners such as general contractors, engineers, suppliers and manufacturers, when needed to price or perform work you have asked about
  • Legal requirements, when required by law, subpoena or to protect our rights, property or safety
  • Business transfers, if the company is merged, acquired or its assets are sold

04Cookies and third-party services

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies ourselves. It loads typefaces from Google Fonts and shows an embedded Google Map of our office. Both mean your browser connects to Google, which may receive your IP address and may set its own cookies under the Google Privacy Policy. If we add analytics or other tools in the future, we will update this policy.

05Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request or project. Message and data rates may apply. We do not share mobile numbers or text messaging consent with third parties for their marketing. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

06Data retention and security

We keep inquiry and project records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, tax, warranty and licensing requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

07Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, state law may give you additional rights. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

08Children's privacy

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

09Links to other sites

This site may link to websites we do not operate. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.
