How it works

Our process

  1. Site visit

    We look at the space, the existing equipment, the ductwork and the electrical service.

  2. Sizing and proposal

    A load calculation sets the right capacity, and you receive a written scope and price.

  3. Installation

    Our own crews install the system, protect the property and remove the old equipment.

  4. Start-up and handover

    We commission the system, show you how it works and register the warranty.

Questions

Residential AC: common questions.

Do you work on homes as well as commercial buildings?

Yes. We install, repair and maintain residential air conditioning throughout Miami-Dade and Broward, alongside our commercial work.

What size AC does my house need?

It depends on more than square footage. We calculate the cooling load using your home's layout, insulation, windows and orientation, then recommend the right capacity.

Do you offer financing or discounts?

New AC unit installations currently receive 20% off the installation. Ask us about current offers and terms when you request a quote.
