AC maintenance in South Florida that prevents the breakdown.

South Florida air conditioners run almost all year. Regular maintenance keeps them efficient, catches small problems early and protects the equipment you have invested in, at home or across a commercial property.

What we do

What a maintenance visit covers.

A documented inspection, not a quick look.

01

Coil cleaning

Evaporator and condenser coils cleaned so the system can move heat properly.

02

Refrigerant check

Pressures and temperatures measured to confirm the charge is correct.

03

Electrical inspection

Capacitors, contactors, connections and motor readings tested.

04

Drain line service

Drain lines and pans cleared to prevent leaks and water damage.

05

Filters and airflow

Filters replaced and airflow checked across the system.

06

Commercial maintenance agreements

Scheduled visits, written reports and budget planning for property managers.

Good to know

Why maintenance pays for itself

  • Fewer emergency breakdowns in peak heat
  • Lower energy use from clean coils and correct charge
  • Longer equipment life
  • Problems found before they become expensive
  • Documentation for warranties and property records
  • Predictable costs for commercial budgets

  1. Schedule

Questions

AC Maintenance: common questions.

How often should AC be serviced in South Florida?

Because systems here run most of the year, we recommend maintenance at least twice a year for homes, and quarterly or more for most commercial equipment.

Do you offer commercial maintenance contracts?

Yes. We provide planned maintenance agreements for offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses and multi-site portfolios, with scheduled visits and documented reports.

Does maintenance include repairs?

The visit covers inspection, cleaning and testing. If we find a part that needs repair, we explain it and give you a price before doing any additional work.
