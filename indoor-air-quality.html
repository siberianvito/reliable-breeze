Good to know

Signs of an air quality problem

  • Rooms that feel damp or sticky
  • Musty odors when the AC runs
  • Visible dust shortly after cleaning
  • Allergy symptoms indoors
  • Condensation on vents or windows
  • Uneven airflow between rooms

Questions

Indoor Air Quality: common questions.

Why is humidity such a problem in South Florida?

Warm, humid outdoor air enters buildings constantly. If the air conditioning is oversized or airflow is wrong, it cools without removing enough moisture, leaving rooms cold but damp.

Can a new filter really make a difference?

Yes, when it is matched to the system. A higher-efficiency filter captures more particles, but it has to be selected so it does not restrict airflow.

Do you handle air quality for commercial buildings?

Yes. We design and install ventilation and filtration for offices, healthcare spaces and other commercial buildings.
