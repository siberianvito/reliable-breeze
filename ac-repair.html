Good to know

Signs you need AC repair

  • The system runs but the air is not cold
  • Rooms that will not cool evenly
  • Water leaking near the indoor unit
  • Grinding, buzzing or rattling noises
  • A sudden jump in the electric bill
  • The unit turns on and off repeatedly

Questions

AC Repair: common questions.

Do you offer emergency AC repair?

Yes. Our emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for both commercial and residential customers. Call (954) 775-8248.

What areas do you cover for AC repair?

Will I know the cost before the repair?

Yes. After diagnosing the system we explain what we found and give you a price before any repair work begins.
