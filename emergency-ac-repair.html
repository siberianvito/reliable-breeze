Nights, weekends and holidays. If cooling or refrigeration is down, call.

03

Refrigeration emergencies

Walk-in coolers, freezers and cold storage that cannot hold temperature.

04

Water leaks

Overflowing drain pans and leaks that threaten ceilings, floors and equipment.

05

Electrical and burning smells

Systems that trip breakers or smell hot should be shut off and checked.

06

Critical spaces

Server rooms, healthcare spaces and other areas that cannot go without cooling.

Good to know

What to do while you wait

  • Turn the system off at the thermostat if you smell burning or see ice
  • Check that the breaker has not tripped
  • Check the air filter and replace it if it is clogged
  • Close blinds and limit heat sources indoors
  • Move perishable stock if refrigeration is failing
  • Have the unit location and access details ready
