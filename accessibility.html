Accessibility Statement

Effective November 14, 2026

Reliable Breeze Air Conditioning & Refrigeration wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people with disabilities. We are working to make the site meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

01What we have done

  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators and a skip-to-content link
  • Labeled form fields and clear error messages
  • Support for the "reduce motion" setting on your device
  • A layout that works at phone, tablet and desktop sizes and when zoomed

02Accessibility menu

The round button at the bottom right of every page opens an accessibility menu. It lets you enlarge text, increase contrast, highlight links, switch to a more readable font, add text spacing, pause animations and enlarge the cursor. Your choices are remembered on your device.

The menu is an added convenience. It does not replace the accessibility built into the site itself, and it does not replace assistive technology such as a screen reader.

03Known limitations

The scroll-driven visuals on the home page are decorative, and all of their information is also provided as text. Some generated images are illustrative. If any part of the site is difficult for you to use, please tell us.

04Feedback and assistance

If you have trouble using this site, or need information in another format, contact us and we will help you directly. We aim to respond within two business days.
